Every social item below is an unpublished draft derived from the approved public content. Confirm the destination is live and the evidence/price wording still matches before posting. No post, outreach, profile edit, or monitoring schedule has been executed.

Each week: Monday review the prior week's normalized funnel and delivery errors; Tuesday check content/source accuracy and choose one question from real inquiries; Wednesday edit and approve the draft; Thursday publish manually if authorized; Friday review inquiry quality and document one next action. Reuse the LinkedIn draft with the short Instagram caption and the approved visual/portfolio asset, adding the corresponding destination link through the account's existing linking convention. For each 28-day comparison, use complete days and identical property, search type, country/device filters, and time zone. Record publication dates, instrumentation changes, consent rate, and outages so that differences are interpretable. Search Console's AI feature appearances are included within Web reporting; an AI visibility test is a separate diagnostic and is not a lead baseline. Do not attribute revenue to an AI tool merely because a referrer is absent.

Maintain two visibility prompt cohorts in `prompt-cohorts.json`. The original specification and its exact historical prompts were not found in this checkout. Until supplied, the historical baseline cohort is intentionally empty and marked as an external dependency. Preserve the original sample and wording when available; do not substitute these new prompts into its time series. The proposed expansion cohort covers new service/use-case/geographic destinations, has its own prospective baseline, and must be reported separately.

For each authorized manual prompt sample, record prompt ID/version, date, model/platform/version, locale, browsing setting, signed-in state, repetition count, exact answer, KDH mention, cited KDH URL, source accuracy, and competitor presence. Keep historical visibility results apart from inquiries and revenue. Run the same frozen cohort/settings on days 0, 28, 56, and 84 where access permits. No sampling or scheduled monitoring was performed in this implementation.

At days 60–90, the business owner decides whether the volume and quality of inquiries, consultations, proposals, and revenue justify more geographic content. Use real commercial outcomes and operational capacity; do not generate a page for every town by default.
